What AI Observed

  • Circular brown spots with concentric dark rings.
  • Yellow halo surrounding the affected tissue.
  • Lower leaves showing advanced drying and defoliation.

Possible Causes

  • High temperature (25-30°C) coupled with high humidity (84%).
  • Extended periods of leaf wetness from over-irrigation.
  • Spores spread by wind or splash water from infected soil.

Recommended Next Steps

  1. Inspect nearby plants to gauge outbreak extent.
  2. Prune infected lower leaves and safely destroy them away from field.
  3. Avoid sprinkler or overhead irrigation. Drip irrigation is highly recommended.
  4. Check local weather. Spraying should only be done in dry conditions.
